# Reference data: Pack Manager

> ⚠️ **DUMMY DATA.** Every row is synthetic, generated by a seeded script. The SKUs, ASINs, FNSKUs, orders, suppliers, operators, requirement flags and money amounts are all invented. Nothing here is a real Amazon rule or fee. Don't train on it, and don't treat it as ground truth.

**File:** `pack_sample.csv` (29 rows)

Use it to design your schema, your structured output and your record page, and to see how your stage connects to the others through `unit_id`. Your **eval set is separate**: 50 units your agent has never seen, labelled independently by two humans.

The values `uncertain` and `pending_review` appear on purpose. Engineering rule 4 treats *uncertain* as a first-class verdict.

## Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `record_id` | This stage's record ID. The prefix identifies the stage: RCV, PRP, PCK, RTN. |
| `unit_id` | The unit this record is about. It's the same ID in all five repos, which is the join key for the chain. |
| `org_id` | Tenant. `org_demo_alpha` or `org_demo_bravo`. Use the two orgs for your isolation test. |
| `photo_refs` | `;`-separated placeholder image paths. **No images are included.** Capture your own fixtures. |
| `operator_id` | The person who made the call (dummy). |
| `captured_at` | UTC timestamp of the capture. |
| `order_id` | Buyer order (dummy). |
| `channel` | `amazon_mfn`, `shopify`, `walmart`, `3pl_client`. Never FBA: Amazon packs those boxes itself. |
| `order_lines` | What should be in the box, as `SKU:qty;SKU:qty`. |
| `observed_in_box` | What was actually in the open box, in the same format. Some rows deliberately differ: missing item, short quantity, extra item, wrong item. |
| `operator_verdict` | What the human decided: `seal` or `stop_and_fix`. It is sometimes wrong on purpose. |

Found something inconsistent? That's a finding. Open an Issue labelled `finding`.
