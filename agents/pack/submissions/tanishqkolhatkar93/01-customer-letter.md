# Working Backwards: Pack Manager AI

Dear Fulfillment Partners,

Today, we are thrilled to announce the launch of **Pack Manager AI**, a revolutionary quality assurance tool designed to eliminate outbound shipping errors before the box is even taped shut.

For years, 3PLs and independent sellers have struggled with the costly reality of mispacked boxes. A missing item or wrong SKU leads to frustrated customers, expensive return shipping, and vendor chargebacks. Traditional barcode scanning is slow and doesn't verify the physical presence of the item in the box.

Pack Manager AI solves this by introducing a seamless, instantaneous visual check at the packing station. By taking a top-down photograph of the open box, our system compares the visual contents against the expected order manifest in real-time. If the box is perfect, the operator is given the green light to seal it. If there is a missing, extra, or incorrect item, the system immediately flags a "STOP & FIX", complete with a cryptographic evidence receipt of the error.

Our promise is simple: Faster line speeds, zero unverified outbound errors, and cryptographic proof of every package you ship.

Welcome to the future of fulfillment.
